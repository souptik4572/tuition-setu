## Found and fixed

1. **A message about the engine stopping mid-write was generic.** It said "Something went wrong while writing". When the connection drops mid-reply, Node's `fetch` reports `TypeError: terminated`, and the app didn't map that to "the engine is gone". Now it does, so the screen says "The AI engine stopped partway through. Your note is safe and nothing was sent." (`lib/ai/errors.ts`, with a test)
2. **A score showed as "unknown 18/25".** When the note didn't name the test ("Tanmoy fractions 18/25"), Gemma filled the name in with "unknown". The validate step (S5) now calls such a score a "test". (`lib/pipeline/validate.ts`, with a test)
3. **A sent update still said "Preview · not sent"** under the message on the review screen. It now says "Sent Sat 17 Oct, 11:02 AM". (`components/ts/message-bubble.tsx`, `app/cycles/[id]/review.tsx`)
4. **`./setup.sh` printed 12 lines of npm warnings** (deprecated packages, install scripts) that look like errors to a tutor. npm now prints only errors there. (`setup.sh`)
5. **A note typed after "Complete week" made a second copy of the same week.** With no open week, a new one was made around the note's date. A tutor who completes the week on Sunday (report day, the week's last day) and types a note that evening got a second "12–18 Oct". Now the next week starts after the last one, as the done page promises ("Next week starts Mon 19 Oct"). Found while recording the demo video. (`lib/db/queries/cycles.ts`, with a test)

## Seen, working as designed

- Gemma's advice sometimes includes numbers that aren't in the notes, such as "practise 2-3 short word problems". The guard flags them (G1), and the tutor dismisses the warning or edits the text.
- Every Bengali and Hindi update has a "check the name" warning (G2), because the model writes the name in the parent's script. This is a warning, not a flag (IMPL M9).
